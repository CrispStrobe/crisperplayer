Build 15: custom sleep timers and reliable expiry.

Open the sleep-timer sheet from the player and test a custom duration: enter 1–1440 minutes and press Enter or Start custom timer. Reopen the sheet and check that the last custom duration is remembered. Invalid values must leave playback and the current timer unchanged.

While a timer is active, test +5 minutes and +15 minutes. Extensions must add to the remaining time and preserve “finish the chapter” mode, including when already waiting for a chapter boundary. Cancel must prevent a later automatic pause.

Test timer expiry with the screen locked and after returning from the background. A delayed callback must not restart a full countdown. Immediate expiry pauses and saves progress; chapter-end mode finishes the chapter playing when the timer expires. Timers do not survive app termination and are canceled when changing books.

Regression checks: local M4B/multi-track imports, chapter navigation, headset/lock-screen controls, bookmarks, resume, queue, speed, editable elapsed time, volume/mute restoration, and generating a measured waveform while playback continues. Check the sleep sheet and player with VoiceOver and enlarged text.

Offline NAS libraries support Plex, WebDAV, SFTP and SMB downloads. Cloud/progress sync is unavailable. JSON metadata exports do not include audio. Requires iOS 15 or newer.
