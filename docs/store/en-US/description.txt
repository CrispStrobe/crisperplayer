Listen to your own audiobooks and pick up where you left off.

Import audio from Files and other apps, or download books from Plex, Filen, Internxt, Nextcloud, WebDAV, SFTP and SMB for offline listening.

YOUR LIBRARY
Organize books into series and collections. Edit titles, authors and covers, mark books finished, and keep an Up next queue.

YOUR PLAYER
Navigate embedded chapters, add bookmarks and notes, change playback speed, adjust volume, or tap the timecode to jump to a precise position. Generate a measured waveform locally for supported audio.

LISTEN COMFORTABLY
Background audio and system media controls keep your book available with the screen locked. Smart rewind, configurable skips and custom sleep timers help you continue comfortably.

KEEP YOUR PLACE
Optionally sync listening positions with Plex or use encrypted progress files in a folder on your own Nextcloud/WebDAV, Filen or Internxt account. Offline progress stays local until a connection is available. Conflicting changes require a choice.

LOCAL FIRST
No CrisperPlayer account, advertising or tracking. Your imported audio stays in your library. Optional connections use your own server/provider accounts. Export library metadata for a separate backup.

Supports M4B/M4A, MP3, FLAC, Ogg, Opus, WAV and other formats supported by your device. Format support depends on the platform and file encoding.

CrisperPlayer is open source under AGPL-3.0. Source, support and privacy information are available through the app's About dialog.
