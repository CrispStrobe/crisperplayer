CrisperPlayer direct native desktop audio
========================================

Windows/Linux use Dart transport and bounded Glint decoding, the shared native
Sonic/Chromium WSOLA/Signalsmith Stretch/Bungee Basic processors, and miniaudio
PCM output. No libmpv or FFmpeg runtime is linked or shipped by this audio path.
The shared DSP source and original grants are in ANDROID-DSP.txt (historical
filename). Unrelated system/application libraries retain their own licenses.

Glint decoder sources
---------------------
https://github.com/CrispStrobe/glint/tree/7b458cff0fc9c397f671c422db4770c72fc22f5c
Exact selected files, original/current hashes and local decoder patches:
packages/crisper_audio/native/vendor/glint/UPSTREAM.json and UPSTREAM.patch

Glint declares MIT. The retained AAC-table generator describes extraction and
cross-checking against vo-aacenc (Apache-2.0) and FFmpeg libavcodec without exact
extraction commits. Its derived-table attribution/license treatment remains
unresolved, as also recorded by CrispAudio. The MIT declaration is not treated
as blanket distribution clearance. No FFmpeg implementation is linked here.
