Desktop audio components

Windows uses media_kit_libs_windows_audio 1.0.9 and its libmpv/FFmpeg build.
The plugin pins the mpv-dev-x86_64-20230924-git-652a1dd.7z binary:
https://github.com/media-kit/libmpv-win32-audio-build/releases/tag/2023-09-24
Plugin source and download/checksum configuration:
https://github.com/media-kit/media-kit/tree/main/libs/windows/media_kit_libs_windows_audio
Builder source and build configuration:
https://github.com/media-kit/libmpv-win32-audio-build
libmpv source: https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv
FFmpeg source: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html

The audio builder disables GPL components in mpv and FFmpeg. libmpv is used
under LGPL-2.1-or-later; FFmpeg's enabled version-three components require
LGPL-3.0-or-later. Their full license texts are included in this directory,
including GPL-3.0 as incorporated by LGPL-3.0. Individual upstream components
retain their copyrights and applicable licenses. See upstream build recipes
and source for component-specific notices. This notice grants no new rights.

Linux uses system libmpv and its codec dependencies rather than including
those libraries in the release archive; install your distribution's packages.
Android, iOS and macOS use their native playback backends.

The app bundles its desktop libraries alongside the executable. For LGPL
components, you may replace compatible shared libraries with modified builds;
keep their required ABI and file names. Reverse engineering for debugging
modifications to LGPL components is permitted under their upstream licenses.
